# CMA (ניתוח שוק השוואתי) Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Eyal uploads Madlan deal tables once a month. From them and our own deals, Effie computes a recommended price range for an owner's property. The result appears as a blurred-range "teaser" page inside the presentation kit, and as a full view for the agent at the meeting.

**Architecture:**
- A new module `cma.py` holds:
  - pure functions: Madlan file parsing and cleaning, street canonicalisation, linking our deals, picking comparables and computing the range
  - `cma_block(G, listing)`, the hook the kit calls
  - `register(app, G)` with the upload routes, the agent-view routes and the standalone agent screen
- One Supabase table, `market_deals`, reached through new `market_*` functions in `supabase_db.py`.
- Small hunks elsewhere:
  - `app.py`: the register block
  - `effie_v2.py`: the admin card and a drawer item
  - the kit files (`kit_render._page_cma`, the review line, the `/v2/kits` button), and only after the kit chat reports that its build is done

**Tech Stack:** Flask, openpyxl through the existing `effie_v2.ibs_parse_table`, `statistics.quantiles`, Pillow + python-bidi + Heebo (kit renderer), Supabase REST.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-06-cma-design.md` (approved by Eyal 06/11). Read it first. The exact rules are in §3–§7, and the kit contract is in §8. Kit plan: `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-11-06-presentation-kit.md`.

## Global Constraints
- **No AI calls.** Everything is deterministic.
- **Sale, residential only.** Skip rent and commercial in Madlan rows, our deals and listings.
- **Window: 3 years back from today**, as Israel date.
- **Comparables.**
  - Same type. "דירה" equals "דירה בבית קומות". דירת גן, פנטהאוז/גג, דופלקס and קוטג' only match their own type.
  - Same rooms, then ±1 if there aren't enough. 3.5 matches 3, 3.5 and 4.
  - Area within ±20% of the subject.
  - Order: same house first, then nearest house number on the same street (ties: newest first), then the rest of the neighbourhood (newest first).
  - **Up to 7, minimum 4.**
- **Outlier:** a price per m² more than 30% away from the median of the chosen comparables is dropped and the next candidate takes its place.
- **Range:** `statistics.quantiles(ppm, n=4, method='inclusive')` gives q1 and q3. Multiply each by the subject's m² and round to 10,000. Without m², use the quantiles of the deal prices with no area filter, and set `basis:"price"`.
- **Bad prices:** a price under 150,000 ₪ or a price per m² under 5,000 is never used, but it is still stored.
- **Our deal = a Madlan row** on the same street and house, with price within ±1% and date within ±21 days. It is counted once, gets rooms, m² and floor from Madlan, and is flagged `ours`.
- **Teaser numbers (owner-facing).**
  - `street_count`: every deal on the street with the same type and the exact rooms, over 3 years, after cleaning. If it is below 3, count the neighbourhood instead and set `level:"neighborhood"`.
  - `ppm_median`: the median of that same set, rounded to 100.
  - `competing`: active sale listings on the same street with the same type and rooms ±1. Exclude the subject's own listing and duplicates of the same flat.
- **The owner-facing page never shows** deal rows, house numbers, the word "מדלן", "עסקה שלנו", or the real range. The range is drawn as fake digits under blur.
- **Privacy:** never read `client_name`, `client_phone` or commission columns from `deal_history`.
- **Palette** (closed): navy `#1E3A5F`, cream `#F2EFE7`, gold `#C29435`/`#E4C56B`, text on gold `#231700`, muted `#6B7280`/`#5B6472`, white cards. Heebo, RTL, no emojis. "אפי" never appears.
- **Commits:** local only, and only your own files. Run `git pull --ff-only` first. Other chats have uncommitted work in `app.py` / `effie_v2.py`, so use `git add -p` and check `git diff --cached`. Never commit the two real Madlan xlsx files; tests use a small synthetic xlsx built in the test.
- **Tests:** pytest for pure functions; plain-script `ck()` style for routes (copy `tests/social_video/test_routes.py`); JS: `node --check` plus node on the real function. There's no `timeout` command on the Mac.

## Review Focus
1. **A wrong file.** A Madlan export with columns in another order, a non-Madlan xlsx, or a CSV is rejected with a clear reason and nothing is saved. Columns are mapped by header, not position. Task 1 test.
2. **Subject with no house number, or a street that is in no file.** It doesn't crash. With no house number, the same-street deals are ordered by date. With a street in no file, there is no neighbourhood level. If there are too few comparables, it returns None. Task 2 test.
3. **The same deal twice.** It appears twice in one file, in two neighbourhood files, or in both Madlan and our `deal_history`. It is counted once everywhere: the teaser count, the median and the comparables. Task 1 and Task 2 tests.
4. **Odd values.** Rooms "3.5", m² empty or 0, a price like "1,800,007", and project-name addresses ("Dori Dream קרית מוצקין") don't crash. Rows without m² are skipped only where m² is needed. Task 1 and Task 2 tests.
5. **The real range never reaches the owner.** The owner view HTML of `/k/<tok>` and the rendered CMA image (OCR is not needed; check the snapshot path) don't contain `lo`/`hi`. The fake blurred digits are not derived from the real ones. Task 4 test.

  - `market_upsert(rows: list) -> int` uses `_upsert_bulk("market_deals", rows, key_field="deal_key")`. Existing keys are left untouched: use `Prefer: resolution=ignore-duplicates`, so a deal keeps the neighbourhood of the first file it came in.

- `cma.py`:
  - `MADLAN_HEADERS = ("יום מכירה","כתובת","מחיר (₪)","סוג נכס","חדרים","שטח (מ״ר)","קומה","מחיר (₪) למ״ר","שנת בנייה")`
  - `canon_street(name: str, known: set[str]) -> str`. It normalises קריית→קרית, quote characters and extra spaces. It returns a `known` name when the tokens match either directly, as a rotation ("בגין מנחם"→"מנחם בגין"), or after dropping a leading ה, or when exactly one known name ends with the input ("בן צבי"→"יצחק בן צבי"). Otherwise it returns the normalised input.
  - `parse_madlan_addr(s: str) -> {"street","house","city","project"}`. `"בגין מנחם 12, קרית מוצקין"` gives street "בגין מנחם", house 12, city "קרית מוצקין". A part with no number (a project name or "ללא רחוב") gives street "", house None and the project text.
  - `madlan_rows(table: list[dict], nb: str, file_name: str, known: set) -> tuple[list[dict], dict]`. It returns the rows for `market_upsert` and `stats {n, dups, bad, min_date, max_date, streets}`. It raises `ValueError("headers")` when any of `MADLAN_HEADERS` is missing.
  - `deal_key(r) -> str`: `date|street|house|price|rooms|sqm|floor`, with project as the street when there is none.
  - `mark_dup_projects(rows) -> rows`. When the same (date ±3 days, price, rooms, sqm, floor) appears under more than one address, only rows that have a street and house are kept, and they are kept only when there is exactly one such row. Every other row in the group gets `bad="dup_project"`.

### Task 2: Linking our deals and computing the CMA

**Files:**
- Modify: `cma.py` (the pure calculation part)
- Test: `tests/cma/test_calc.py` (pytest)

**Interfaces:**
- Consumes: Task 1 row shape.
- Produces:
  - `ours_rows(dh_rows: list, known: set) -> list`. `deal_history` rows go to the market row shape with `source="ours"`.
    - Keep sale sides only: drop שוכר and משכיר. Also drop rows with `missing_since` set and future dates.
    - Parse street and house with the deals_history address helpers. The city is the canonical form of `deals_history`'s city ("ק. מוצקין" becomes "קרית מוצקין").
    - The two sides of one deal (same street, house, price and date within 14 days) collapse into one row.
    - Never read client or commission columns.
  - `link_ours(market: list, ours: list) -> list`. Returns the merged list. Matched pairs become one row: the Madlan values plus `ours=True`. Unmatched rows from `ours` are kept, with `rooms` and `sqm` None and `ours=True`.
  - `subject_from_listing(listing: dict) -> dict|None`. Reads tolerantly:
    - street: `רחוב`/`רחוב1`/`כתובת`/`street`
    - house: `מספר בית`/`house`, or parsed from the street
    - city: `עיר / ישוב`/`עיר`/`city`
    - rooms: `חדרים`/`rooms`
    - sqm: `sqm`/`מ"ר`
    - ptype: `ptype`/`סוג נכס`, defaulting to "דירה"
    - price: `מחיר`/`price`
    - key: `listing_key`/`key`
    - It returns None without a street or rooms.
  - `subject_nb(street: str, city: str, deals: list) -> str` returns the most frequent `nb` among deals on that street, or "".
  - `cma_compute(subject: dict, deals: list, listings: list, today: date) -> dict|None`. `deals` is the output of `link_ours`. `listings` are records shaped like `effie_v2.crm_props_records` output. It returns None when there are fewer than 4 comparables. Otherwise it returns the contract:
    ```
    {"title":"כמה שווה הנכס שלך","subject":{street,house,city,rooms,sqm,ptype},
     "level":"street"|"neighborhood","street_count":int,"since_year":int,"ppm_median":int,
     "competing":int,"range":[lo,hi],"ppm_range":[q1,q3],"basis":"sqm"|"price","n_used":int,
     "comps":[{street,house,date,rooms,sqm,floor,price,ppm,ours,used,reason}],
     "listings":[{source,street,house,rooms,sqm,price}],"data_as_of":"YYYY-MM-DD"}
    ```
    - `reason` is "" for used comps, or "גדולה/קטנה מדי", "חריג", "מחוץ לטווח הזמן".
    - `comps` lists the candidates in order, both used and dropped, up to 12.
    - `data_as_of` is the latest `uploaded_at` among the subject's nb rows.
